Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how WMC Executive Private Hire (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses and looks after personal information when you use our website at wmcprivatehire.com or contact us about our services.

We are an executive private hire service based in Northampton, Northamptonshire, United Kingdom. Journeys are operated in partnership with First Class Chauffeuring Limited, which is licensed by West Northamptonshire Council under Licence No LN/202600406.

2. How to contact us

If you have a question about this policy or about your personal information, please contact us:

  • Phone or WhatsApp: +44 7459 417049
  • Email: help@wmcprivatehire.com

3. Personal information we collect

We collect information that you choose to give us, and some limited technical information that is created when you use the website.

Enquiries and bookings

When you use the booking, enquiry or review form on this website, the details you enter (for example your name, email address, telephone number, journey details, a star rating and review text) are used to open a WhatsApp message addressed to us. We receive that information when you send the message. The form does not itself store a copy of your enquiry in a database on this website.

If you contact us by telephone, WhatsApp or email, we will process the information you provide so that we can reply, provide a quote and, where applicable, arrange and carry out a booking.

Quote calculator

If you use our online quote calculator, the pickup and drop-off addresses you type are sent to our website server so that we can estimate the distance and an indicative fare. To do this, our server uses OpenStreetMap services (geocoding and routing) to locate the addresses and calculate a route. Addresses you enter are therefore shared with those OpenStreetMap services for that purpose.

Technical information

Our website server may keep logs that include technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages or files requested. We use this information to operate, secure and troubleshoot the website. We do not use Google Analytics or similar advertising or analytics tools on this website.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics cookies on this website, and we do not use tools such as Google Analytics or Meta (Facebook) Pixel.

Essential cookies may be used for the secure administration area of the website. Ordinary visits to our public pages do not rely on advertising or tracking cookies.

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. The contact page also includes an embedded map provided by Google Maps, showing Northamptonshire. Google may collect technical data or set its own cookies when those services load, in accordance with Google’s own privacy policy. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and provide quotes;
  • confirm, manage and carry out bookings;
  • communicate with you about your journey, including by telephone, WhatsApp or email;
  • operate, secure and improve the website; and
  • meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Legal bases for processing

We process personal information in accordance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Depending on the circumstances, our legal bases are:

  • Contract — to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and to perform a booking once it is confirmed;
  • Legitimate interests — to operate and secure our business and website, and to respond to general enquiries, where those interests are not overridden by your rights;
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep or disclose information to comply with the law; and
  • Consent — where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time by contacting us.

7. Who we share information with

We only share personal information where it is needed to provide our service or where the law requires it. This may include:

  • Our licensed partner operator, First Class Chauffeuring Limited, where this is necessary to operate a booked journey;
  • WhatsApp / Meta, when you send us an enquiry or booking message through WhatsApp. WhatsApp’s use of your data is governed by Meta’s own terms and privacy policy;
  • OpenStreetMap services, when you use the quote calculator, as described above;
  • Google, for fonts used on the website and for the map embed on the contact page; and
  • professional advisers, insurers, or public authorities if we are required or permitted to do so by law.

8. How long we keep information

We keep booking and enquiry information only for as long as we need it to provide the service, handle queries, and meet legal or accounting requirements. We then delete or anonymise it. Server logs are kept only for as long as is reasonably needed for security and the operation of the website.

9. How we look after your information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right, in certain circumstances, to:

  • request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to erase your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your information in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent, where we rely on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in section 2. We may need to verify your identity before we can respond. You will not usually have to pay a fee, unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

11. Complaints

We hope we can resolve any concern you have about how we use your information. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the supervisory authority for data protection in the United Kingdom. You can find out more at ico.org.uk.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the date of the last update shown at the top.
